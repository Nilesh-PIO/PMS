# Module 05 — Search & Navigation

Source: `BRD/Doc_BRD.md` → Functional Requirements → Search & Navigation.

## Requirements

- Quick patient search.
- View recent patients.
- Easy navigation between patient profile and visits.

## Relevant success criteria

- Patient search and history retrieval within 2–5 seconds (see [00-overview.md](00-overview.md)).

## Related modules

- [01-patient-management.md](01-patient-management.md) — search operates over patient records created there.
- [04-patient-history.md](04-patient-history.md) — navigation connects a patient's profile to their visit history.

## Notes

The BRD doesn't define what "quick search" matches on (prefix, substring, phone digits), how many "recent patients" are shown, or whether "recent" means recently viewed or recently seen in consultation. See `doc/brainstorm-pms-verification.md` and `doc/planning-pms-verification.md` for the adopted definitions.
